Roblox DevEx Explained: Rates, Requirements, and Examples

DevEx is the bridge between Roblox creator earnings and real-world money. The mistake most new creators make is treating every Robux balance like cash. Only eligible Earned Robux matters.

What is Earned Robux?

Earned Robux generally means Robux you receive from eligible creator activity such as monetizing experiences, passes, developer products, and other approved creator systems.
