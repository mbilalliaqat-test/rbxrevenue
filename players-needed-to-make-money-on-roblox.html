How Many Players Do You Need to Make Money on Roblox?

There is no magic player count. A small game with strong conversion can outperform a bigger game with weak monetization.

The core formula

Players needed = buyers needed ÷ buyer conversion rate

Example

If each buyer is worth about $0.80 and your target is $1,000, you need around 1,253 buyers. At a 3% conversion rate, that means roughly 41,767 active players.
