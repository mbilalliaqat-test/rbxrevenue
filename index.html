SEO FAQ

What is the Roblox DevEx Calculator?

It is a creator revenue calculator that estimates how much Earned Robux may be worth after applying a DevEx rate. It also helps estimate product revenue, costs, profit, and player targets.

How much is 30,000 Earned Robux in DevEx?

At a DevEx rate of $0.0038 per Earned Robux, 30,000 Earned Robux equals an estimated $114 before tax, eligibility, and policy considerations.

Does Roblox take a fee from gamepass sales?

For passes sold inside your own experience, the default calculator assumes a 70% creator share. You can adjust this field if your situation is different.

How many Robux do I need to make $100?

At $0.0038 per Earned Robux, you would need about 26,316 Earned Robux to estimate $100. This does not guarantee eligibility or final payout.

Can this calculator guarantee my Roblox payout?

No. It is only an estimation tool. Your real payout can depend on Roblox policies, DevEx eligibility, tax forms, account status, timing, location, transaction type, and future rate changes.
